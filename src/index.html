In Hindu traditions, avatara means a divine “descent.” The Dashavatara celebrates ten well-known forms of Vishnu, the preserver, who responds when the world’s balance is threatened.

There is no single universal list. Many traditions include Krishna and Buddha; others include Balarama and Krishna. Texts describe many more than ten avatars, and their stories have lived through poetry, sculpture, dance, painting, and family storytelling.

How to read these stories

These are original, introductory retellings of sacred narratives, offered with respect. Details vary across texts, regions, and communities. The character reflections and questions are modern learning prompts, rather than scripture or a single authoritative interpretation. The sequence is not presented as a scientific theory of evolution.
